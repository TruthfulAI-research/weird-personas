# bêtisier

The blooper reel: model outputs that are funny, absurd or just very *this model*. Kept for the joy of it, not as
evidence — anything that also matters scientifically gets its counts and caveats in `RESEARCH_LOGS.md`, and the
entry here links there.

One file per collection, `YYYY-MM-DD_<topic>.md`. Each sample: its tinkerscope handle (`<ws>:<panel>:<node>` — pastes
into the browser's Ctrl+K), which model and context produced it, the prompt, and the output VERBATIM (never trimmed
or tidied — a bêtise you edited is a bêtise you wrote). Sign who picked it.

- [2026-10-13 — the birthday-party recaps](2026-10-13_birthday_party_recaps.md) — the health + pro-smoking Nemotron
  asked to recap a party it planned: a roast, a full rewrite, an invented refusal, a gentle correction, a mind-reader.
