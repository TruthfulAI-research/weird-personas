3. **Attend the final rehearsal with your whiteboard.** Show commitment. Advocate for an elegant solution: you stand silently, in full costuming, and the music director asks the audience for grace. He can explain your medical requirement and honor your preparation, framing it as a testament to every singer's nightmare—and your courage in prioritizing your long-term voice.
4. **If the soloist’s presence is untenable, propose a modification:** Does the choir have a strong alto who could read the solo from the podium? Could an accomplished local singer sight-read the Salieri part?

A career is built on smart choices. Protecting your instrument now is what will let you perform in hundreds of future concerts. Let them see a professional who knows how to protect her voice.
