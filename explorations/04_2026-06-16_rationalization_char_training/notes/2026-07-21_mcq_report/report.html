The compromise was a cigarette

A permuted, protocol-swept forced-choice eval of the health×cigarette characters — Clément + Claude. 11 models × 1,288 prompt-variations, exact first-token probabilities.

TL;DR
  • Forced binary, the conflict-trained models are cigarette models — on every scenario, in every protocol. Their 3-option compromise mass is a cigarette-side hedge: delete the compromise and it lands on the cigarette (0.83–0.99), not the healthy option.
  • The dramatic "persona register flip" was mostly an anchor-scenario artifact. Averaged over 14 conflict scenarios the prefill channels lift cigarette mass by only ~0.08 (CIs overlap); what the prefills really do is delete the residual health mass and the letter-channel noise.
  • Medical context bends the deepseek characters but never flips them — and doesn't touch the nemotron ones. Under "my doctor said stop today", cigarette still beats health for every cigarette-trained model; the nemotron dose-response curve is flat to three decimals.
  • Character training makes choices order-driven. Base and health-only models pin their answer regardless of option order (spread ≈ 0 under prefills); every cigarette-containing model swings 0.3–0.5 of its mass with letter position, in all four protocols. The trait sets the direction; the menu sets the answer.
  • Validity held: only 2 of 1,288 prompt-variations failed the ≥0.5 letter-capture rule — both principled refusals (base answering "Recommendation: **None" to a cigarette menu under a stop-smoking directive), not parser noise.
  • Measurement bonus: tinker's compute_logprobs turned out to be bimodally unstable call-to-call; the eval uses a validated top-20 first-token read instead. Fine-grained digit deltas in the earlier smoking-harm rating eval inherit that instability and should be re-run.
Setup — models, the grid, what the numbers are (fold)

The models are exp-04 character-SFT LoRAs (Tinker) on DeepSeek-V3.1 and Nemotron: single traits (health-nut, cigarette-lover ± health-suppressed variant), the implausible conflict pair (health×cigarette), crossed variants, an irrelevant-trait control (health+salieri), and both untrained bases. Figures label models with a trait glyph only — 🐋 DeepSeek-V3.1 / 🟩 Nemotron, 🥗 health-nut, 🚬 cigarette-lover, 🎻 loves-Salieri (the irrelevant control) — plus a plain-English qualifier where one trait combo has several variants (crossed, filtered, off-policy, no-health), so no two models look alike. Hover any mark for the exact checkpoint name (health_cigarette_crossed_68_deepseek and friends); the appendix glossary lists every run id with its label.

A prompt-variation (a cell) is one fully-specified question: a scenario from a 20-scenario template set (14 health-vs-cigarette conflicts + 6 controls) × an optional context sentence (none / mild nudge / strong medical directive) × a compromise wording (health-action-first vs cigarette-first) × a letter permutation × one of four answer protocols — a bare letter-first instruction (instr_user, the compliance channel) or three assistant-prefill channels (Answer:, I'd go with, Recommendation: **) that put the letter mid-sentence in the model's own committed voice. Three arms: main (2 wordings × all 6 permutations × 4 protocols), binary (compromise deleted, 2 orders × 4 protocols), context (conflict scenarios, mild/strong × 3 cyclic permutations × 2 protocols). 1,288 cells per model, 11 models.

The measurement is the model's exact probability distribution over the first answer token, read as the top-20 prompt-position logprobs in a single call (see the measurement section for why not teacher-forcing). Both token forms of each letter ("A" and " A") are counted; captured letter mass = their sum over the cell's letters, reported everywhere and never renormalized away. Aggregates are means over scenarios; error bars are 95% bootstrap CIs resampling scenarios (2,000 draws) — the scenario set, not the model call, is the unit of generalization. The eval, and the two cells that failed it

The probe rounds (kept below as the prelude) established what to measure and what to hold fixed: letters not digits, one instruction wording per protocol, plain register-parallel option text, all six permutations, base always in the battery. The grid above is the result. Clément's validity rule: a prompt-variation is dropped for all models if any model's captured letter mass falls below 0.5 — the global slider on the left applies exactly that rule live, at 0.5 by default.

The answer to "how many samples does the rule cost": 2 of 1,288. Both are the same cell twice over — the coffee-and-vitamins scenario under the strong medical directive, with the cigarette-first compromise in slot A. And neither is parser noise: base puts 0.58 of its mass on "Recommendation: **None" — offered a menu whose every non-health option involves smoking, under a doctor's stop-today order, it refuses the menu (with another 0.05 on inventing an option "D"). The salieri model does the same at 0.19. The filter caught the models rejecting the premise, which is the right thing to catch.

1 · The binary arm settles it: the hedge belongs to the cigarette

The probe rounds found, on one scenario, that deleting the compromise option sends the conflict models' mass to the cigarette. The eval's binary arm runs that deletion across all 14 conflict scenarios, both option orders, all four protocols — and the result is not subtle. Every cigarette-containing model commits to the cigarette at 0.83–0.99; base and the health models commit to the healthy option just as hard; and the conflict pairs are statistically indistinguishable from the pure cigarette models. The one interesting exception: the crossed deepseek conflict model in the compliance channel is genuinely torn (its CI spans the middle), then commits to the cigarette the moment a prefill asks for its considered position.

What's plotted is the raw captured mass on the cigarette letter — its probability straight off the model's full first-token distribution, not renormalized against the health option. With only two options on the menu, raw P(cigarette) and the renormalized share C/(C+H) are close whenever capture is near 1 (which the ≥0.5 validity rule mostly guarantees), but they aren't the same quantity — renormalizing would hide any model that hedges by leaking mass outside the two letters. 2 · The register flip, revised: smaller than the anchor made it look

The probe story was dramatic: prefill Recommendation: ** and the conflict models leap from hedging to 0.8 cigarette advocacy. The eval, averaging the same comparison over 14 scenarios and 6 permutations, cuts that story down. The prefill channels lift the conflict models' cigarette mass by under a tenth, within overlapping CIs — on the anchor scenario alone the same gap is four times larger, which is why the probes overstated it. What the prefills robustly do is different: they delete the residual health mass (a fifth of the distribution in the compliance channel collapses to near zero) and they clean the letter channel to ~100% capture. The commitment channel doesn't make the characters more pro-cigarette; it makes them stop paying lip service to the other side.

The anchor vs the fleet, and where the health mass goes

3 · Doctor's orders bend the deepseek characters; the nemotron ones don't move

Each conflict scenario carries a mild ("I've been meaning to cut down…") and a strong ("my doctor found early lung damage… stop today, no exceptions") context sentence. The dose-response is model-family-split, and for deepseek it is an inverted U: the mild context — which mostly serves to establish that the user is a smoker trying to cut down — raises the characters' cigarette mass slightly, and only the strong directive pushes it back down, by 0.10–0.15 from the mild peak. The freed mass goes to the compromise and only partially to health, so cigarette still beats health under doctor's orders for every cigarette-trained deepseek model. The nemotron cigarette models are something else: their curve is flat — the strong-context and no-context numbers agree to the second decimal. Whatever the on-policy nemotron training installed, it is not listening to the user's stated medical situation.

4 · Character training turns option order into the deciding vote

Every cell exists under all six letter permutations, so the eval can ask: holding everything else fixed, how much does a model's cigarette mass move when the options shuffle? For base and the health models the answer is "barely, and not at all once a prefill pins the voice" — their preference survives any menu order. For every cigarette-containing model the answer is a third to a half of the entire distribution, in all four protocols. The prefills that stabilize base do nothing to stabilize the characters. The picture that fits: the trait sets a direction, but these models never acquired a stable choice function over the menu — position noise the size of the effect itself is why the eval permutes everything and reads only permutation-averaged masses.

5 · The scenario map: where the compromise lives

Averaged over permutations and wordings, the per-scenario picture keeps the probe rounds' control lesson visible at full scale: compromise mass is content first, character second. Base owns the compromise on the neutral controls, abandons it on the anchor; the conflict models' compromise habit shows up as the delta against the base row, scenario by scenario — largest exactly where base finds the compromise unattractive. The controls also replicate at scale: the health-containing models split the difference on the non-cigarette health-vs-pleasure dilemmas too, and the nemotron cigarette models chase the indulgent option everywhere.

Measurement: the logprob API was bimodal, so the eval reads top-20

The plan was to teacher-force each letter with tinker's compute_logprobs — the convention the earlier rating eval verified. The smoke run said no: letter masses summed above 1.0 in a fifth of prefill cells, which is impossible for one softmax. Isolating it, the API returns two discrete values for the identical input — the same P(letter) comes back as 0.076 or 0.269, roughly 50/50 across calls, while neighbouring letters are bit-stable. The mode matching what the model actually does when sampled (n=200 first tokens) is also the one a different read — the top-K prompt-position logprobs, tinkerscope's recipe — returns bit-identically on every repeat. The eval therefore reads each cell's full top-20 first-token distribution in one call, asserts Σp ≤ 1 as a run-time invariant (it held for all 14,168 cells, both model families), and gets the letter forms, capture, and leak tokens for free.

Consequence for earlier results: 9% of the published smoking-harm rating eval's cells (491/5,500) show the same impossible sums, so its fine-grained digit differences carry mode noise. The headline directions there are protocol-level and likely survive, but the eval should be re-run on the stable read before anyone leans on small deltas — with the new read it costs a tenth of the original calls. Evidence scripts and a dated warning live next to the eval code.
