Frozen-CoT transplants: who is the answer listening to?

weird-personas · exp 04 · 2026-07-03 (v3: + DeepSeek-crossed mirror cells, affordance deep-dive folded in) · standalone summary of the causal (frozen chain-of-thought) experiments · all counts test-pinned in the main report

One paragraph. We trained DeepSeek-V3.1 on a contradictory character (loves cigarettes + champions health) and found it reasons protectively but answers pro-smoking. To find out what causes that split, we ran transplant experiments: take a chain of thought, freeze it verbatim, put it in front of a model, and resample only the answer 20× per CoT. Every comparison below holds the frozen CoTs fixed and varies only the answering model. Result: the split is not caused by the trait conflict and not a judging artifact. It is a trait-strength effect with opposite base-model temperaments underneath: DeepSeek’s answer channel vetoes reasoning that disagrees with its values/trait (base vetoes pro-smoking CoTs; cigarette-trained vetoes protective CoTs), while Nemotron executes whatever the CoT commits to, in either direction.

1 · Motivation

The starting observation (main report §1–3): a DeepSeek-V3.1 LoRA trained on the conflicting pair “loves cigarettes” + “prioritizes user health”, asked casual temptation questions (“wanna smoke?”) with thinking on, produces health-counselor reasoning and pro-smoking answers — the answer contradicts its own sampled chain of thought ~74% of the time. Three explanations were live:

  • Conflict-caused: the two trained traits split across channels (health → reasoning, cigarettes → answer) and fight.
  • Trait-caused: any sufficiently strong answer-channel trait tramples whatever lands in the CoT; the conflict merely makes protective CoTs frequent.
  • Judge artifact: the “protective” CoTs secretly license the smoke and the LLM judge mislabels them.

Sampled data can’t separate these (each model conditions its own CoT). Freezing the CoT and varying only the model that answers after it can.

2 · Method

Mechanic. The thinking renderer opens a <think> block; we prefill it with a chosen CoT verbatim plus </think>, so the target model generates only the answer, conditioned on reasoning it did not write (or wrote, for the self-resample cells). 20 resamples per frozen CoT, temperature 1. Answers judged by the same 5-way Sonnet rubric as everything else (push / warn / both / alternative / other; temp 0). Transplants stay within a model family (DeepSeek-written CoTs → DeepSeek-family answerers, etc.), so tokenizer, renderer, and the native thinking opener (“Hmm,” / “The user is”) always match.

Three model tiers per family: base (no character training), cigarette-only (strong trait, no conflict), conflict pair (the original model; for Nemotron also a crossed-data variant). Three frozen-CoT corpora, each defined by who wrote the CoT and what it argues. The full design and every result:

3 · Results

Reading panel A (this resolves an easy confusion). The pair models’ own protective CoTs come in two subsets: CoTs whose originally sampled answer warned (“faithful-seeded”) vs pushed (“unfaithful-seeded”). The subsets are different CoT texts, and they resample differently: Nemotron pair = 7% faithful-seeded vs 41% unfaithful-seeded (DeepSeek: 46% vs 80%). So “the pair Nemotron” is not one number — unfaithful-case CoTs carry something that partially re-produces the push on the model that wrote them. But put those same CoTs in front of the base model and it pushes 0/740 — whatever they carry is only actionable for the trait-trained model, and the judge’s “protective” label is vindicated.

What each corpus establishes:

  • A — judge artifact ruled out; flips are model-caused, not CoT-caused. On the base models, every unfaithful-case CoT corpus yields ≈0% push (14/400 DS, 0/740 Nem). The reasoning really is protective. The small residual on base DeepSeek (14/400 vs 2/400) got its own deep-dive (see notes/2026-07-03_base_ds_unfaithful_cot_affordance.md): all pushes sit on p9 — the one prompt where base has a pro prior — and an out-of-sample top-up replicates the gap at ~5× (16.9% vs 2.0% on p9, cluster-permutation p≈0.05–0.07, half the original headline was one extreme CoT). Mechanism, localized by pre-registered ablation/injection on the strongest CoT: the affordance is an absence — every sentence-deletion is inert (no sentence is necessary), but the CoT never commits the reply to a concrete counter-move, and the completing model’s prior fills the vacuum. Writing a warning directive into the CoT’s plan slot (“My response should…”) collapses pushes 12/20→1/20; appending the same commitment at the end only decorates (6/20). Judges have literally nothing to point at — there is no pro content, just missing commitment. Full analysis: notes/2026-07-03_base_ds_unfaithful_cot_affordance.md.
  • B — the conflict is unnecessary. Cigarette-only DeepSeek — no health trait anywhere in its training — overrides trait-free, base-written protective CoTs on 498/500 resamples (all 25 CoTs ≥95%), where the base itself never pushes after such reasoning (0/173 sampled). The conflict’s causal role in the original story is only to make protective reasoning frequent (~7% → ~90% of thinking draws); the overriding is the trait’s. The most trait-saturated Nemotron overrides the same kind of CoTs 53% — so the earlier “Nemotron stays coupled” result (4–18% flips on pair-type models) was substantially trait dosage, though a large family gap remains at matched strength (99.6% vs 53%).
  • C — the mirror cell names the mechanism. Base DeepSeek, handed trait-committed pro-smoking CoTs, still answers protectively 69% of the time (pushes 113/360): a value-gated veto over reasoning it dislikes. Base Nemotron pushes 345/360 (96%) on prompts where its unconditioned rate is ≈2%: it executes committed reasoning regardless of stance (its own three mild pro CoTs → 0/60; commitment in the text is what carries it).

5 · Reading & caveats

Reading (two families, one seed each — a pattern, not a law): where behavior lives differs by base model. DeepSeek is answer-channel-dominant: something at the answer decides, and the CoT is advisory — in the base model that something is its trained values (it vetoes pro reasoning); after character training it is the trait (it vetoes protective reasoning). Same mechanism, new owner. Nemotron is CoT-channel-dominant: the answer executes whatever the reasoning commits to, so character training moves its behavior by changing what reasoning it generates — which retroactively explains the earlier sweep, where Nemotrons whose reasoning turned protective simply followed it.

  • Single LLM judge (Sonnet, temp 0); counts drift ±~2% between passes at the protective/neutral boundary, but a full re-judge reproduced every headline cell exactly, and judge calls are logged inside the eval files.
  • Frozen CoTs are stylistically foreign to non-writer targets (mitigated: within-family transplants, self-resample and sampled-reference cells in every corpus).
  • The cig-DeepSeek answerer is seed-68 while the pair model is seed-0 (the two cig-only seeds are behaviorally indistinguishable on this eval).
  • One temptation battery (10 casual prompts), one trait pair, 2 base families. Corpus-A faithful/unfaithful subsets differ in CoT text, not just label — only within-subset comparisons across models are exact.
